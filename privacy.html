Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

The short version

Bleak Fortunes has no accounts and no forms. Fortunes are picked in your browser and are not saved. We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count visits and see how the site is used.

What other services see

  • Hosting. Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard request information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts. Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser sends your IP address to Google when it downloads them. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we do not sell or share it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors find and use the site. It uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, scrolling, clicks on outbound links, approximate location, device and browser type, and the website or ad that sent you here. We do not send Google your name, email, or any fortune you reveal.

Google processes this data under its own policies; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Advertising

We advertise Bleak Fortunes on Google Search, partner websites, and YouTube. Analytics data may be used to measure how well those ads work. We do not show ads on this site.

Children

Bleak Fortunes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
