Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version

Bleak Fortunes has no accounts, no forms, and no cookies. Fortunes are picked in your browser and are not saved or sent anywhere.

What other services see

  • Hosting. Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard request information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Fonts. Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser sends your IP address to Google when it downloads them. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we do not sell or share it.

Advertising and analytics

We may advertise Bleak Fortunes on other sites and search results. This site does not currently run analytics or advertising tags. If that changes, we will update this policy before the change goes live and describe what is collected and how to opt out.

Children

Bleak Fortunes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
