About Bleak Fortunes

Bleak Fortunes is a small, independent humor website. It does one thing: it gives you a free fortune that is funnier, and gloomier, than the one in your cookie. There is no sign-up, no account, and nothing to buy.

How the fortunes are written

Every fortune on this site is an original line written for Bleak Fortunes. The style is deliberately overwrought: long similes, grand openings, and a letdown at the end. That style is a tribute to the Bulwer-Lytton Fiction Contest, which for decades celebrated the worst possible opening sentence to a novel. We do not copy contest entries; we only borrow the spirit.

What we keep out

Our fortunes are about small, everyday letdowns: slow Wi-Fi, long meetings, lost socks. We do not write fortunes about death, illness, self-harm, money, or legal trouble, because those are not funny to the person reading them.

Is any of this real?

No. Bleak Fortunes is entertainment only. Fortunes are picked at random and cannot predict anything. Please make your real decisions some other way.
